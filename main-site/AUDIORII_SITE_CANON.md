# AudioRii Public Copy Rules

## Canonical product range

The public product range contains seven products:

1. RiiSign
2. RiiLinr
3. RiiMix
4. RiiSpace
5. RiiForm
6. RiiPulsiv
7. RiiSamplr

RiiSampl is the sampling engine, editing environment and library system inside RiiSamplr. It is not presented as a separate public product page.

## Public voice

The website speaks directly to musicians, producers, engineers, studios, creators, testers and partners.

Use direct product language:

- “RiiSign gives you direct control…”
- “Bring every source into one view…”
- “Shape the movement inside sound…”

Do not write internal handover language such as:

- “the face of the family”
- “working direction”
- “control territory”
- “product direction”
- “can support”
- “will support”
- “remains user controlled”
- “not an autonomous producer”
- “public skeleton”
- “in development” for entire site services

Product stages may still be stated honestly beside individual products.

## Rii

Rii is written as `Rii`, not `RII`, in public prose.

Its public expression is:

Reimagined. Responsive. Relational. Resonant. Intelligent.

It is a product philosophy and engineering identity, not a naming-policy explanation.

## Visual language

Flow Arc, Intersect, Circuit Path, Bracket Flow, Diamond Layer, Signal S Curve, Minimal Arc and Protoglyph are visual and motion language—not products.

The next visual pass may use these marks as distinctive product controls, buttons, route indicators and interface motifs.

## Product images

Concept images are presentation assets. Keep the entire composition visible; do not crop the product name, interface or product box at the right edge.
